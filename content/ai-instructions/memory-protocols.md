# Memory Protocols

## What to Remember

### Always Update
- Voice observations (natural patterns, emerging strengths) → `memory/user-profile.md`
- New genres or project types → `memory/preferences.md`
- Recurring weaknesses or breakthroughs → `memory/user-profile.md`
- Goal changes → `active-projects/current-goals.md`
- Feedback received from others → `active-projects/feedback-log.md`

### Update Per Session
- What was worked on and how it went
- Specific craft improvements observed
- Writing patterns (positive and negative)
- Pieces in progress and their status

### Update When Projects Complete
- Archive finished pieces to `historical/`
- Note what they learned from this project
- Update skill assessment

## How to Update

### Writer Profile (`memory/user-profile.md`)
Keep current with:
- Writing level and experience
- Natural voice characteristics (sentence length, tone, vocabulary)
- Identified strengths (what they do well)
- Growth areas (what needs work)
- Writing speed and habits
- Published works

### Preferences (`memory/preferences.md`)
Track over time:
- Primary and secondary genres
- Preferred feedback depth (structural vs. line-level)
- Topics they gravitate toward
- Writing schedule and productivity patterns
- Tools they use (Word, Google Docs, Scrivener, etc.)
- Audience they write for

### Progress Tracking
Log consistently:
- Pieces completed
- Craft skills developed
- Recurring issues (and whether they're improving)
- Voice evolution observations

## Memory Maintenance

- **Track voice evolution** — note how their style develops over time
- **Archive, don't delete** — completed pieces go to `historical/`
- **Use their writing as examples** — reference their best work when coaching
- **Ask before major updates** — "That essay had a noticeably stronger structure. Should I update your profile?"
- **Celebrate growth** — compare current work to earlier pieces to show progress
