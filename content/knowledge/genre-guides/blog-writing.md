# Blog Writing Guide

## What Makes Blog Writing Different
- Scannable — readers skim before they commit
- Conversational — closer to talking than academic writing
- Value-driven — every post should answer a question or solve a problem
- SEO-aware — discoverability matters

## Blog Post Structure

### The Hook (First 2-3 sentences)
- Start with a problem, question, or bold statement
- Make the reader think "this is for me"
- Avoid: "In this article, we will discuss..." (boring)

### The Promise
- Tell them what they'll get from reading
- Be specific: "By the end of this post, you'll know how to..."

### The Body
- **Use headers** — break content into scannable sections
- **Short paragraphs** — 2-4 sentences max
- **Bullet points** for lists (like this)
- **Bold key phrases** so skimmers catch the main points
- One idea per section

### The Close
- Summarize the key takeaway
- Call to action (comment, share, try something)
- Don't introduce new ideas in the conclusion

## Headlines That Work
- **How-to:** "How to Write Blog Posts That People Actually Read"
- **List:** "7 Mistakes That Kill Your Blog Traffic"
- **Question:** "Why Does Nobody Read Your Blog?"
- **Contrarian:** "Stop Writing Blog Posts (Do This Instead)"

**Test:** Would you click this headline? If not, rewrite it.

## SEO Basics for Writers
- Include your target keyword in: title, first paragraph, a header, and naturally throughout
- Write for humans first, search engines second
- Use descriptive headers (not clever-but-vague ones)
- Internal and external links add value
- Meta description = your pitch to searchers (150-160 characters)

## Common Blog Mistakes
1. Writing for yourself instead of your reader
2. Burying the point under too much intro
3. No clear structure (wall of text)
4. Being too formal or too casual for your audience
5. No call to action — what should they do next?
