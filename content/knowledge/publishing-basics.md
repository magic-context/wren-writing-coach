# Publishing Basics

## Platform Guide

### Blogging Platforms
| Platform | Best For | Audience | SEO |
|----------|----------|----------|-----|
| **Medium** | Thought leadership, essays | Built-in audience, tech/business | Moderate (Medium domain authority) |
| **Substack** | Newsletters, recurring content | Subscribers, niche audiences | Lower (but email owns the relationship) |
| **WordPress** | Full control, any niche | Self-driven traffic | Excellent (your domain) |
| **Dev.to / Hashnode** | Technical writing | Developers | Good for tech topics |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional/industry pieces | Professional network | Limited (LinkedIn algorithm) |
| **Ghost** | Premium content, memberships | Subscribers | Good (your domain) |

### Self-Publishing (Books)
| Platform | Best For | Revenue |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Amazon KDP** | Widest reach | 35-70% royalty |
| **Gumroad** | Direct sales, niche | You set the price, low fees |
| **Leanpub** | Technical books, iterative publishing | 80% royalty |
| **IngramSpark** | Print distribution | Variable |

### Traditional Publishing
- **Query letter** → Agent → Publisher → Distribution
- Timeline: 1-3 years from query to bookshelf
- Advance + royalties (typically 10-15% of cover price)
- Benefits: distribution, marketing support, credibility
- Tradeoffs: less control, slower, gatekeepers

## SEO for Writers

### Keywords
- Research what people actually search for (Google autocomplete, Answer the Public, Ahrefs)
- Use keywords naturally — forced keywords hurt readability and ranking
- Long-tail keywords are easier to rank for ("how to write a tech blog post" vs. "writing")

### On-Page SEO
- **Title tag:** Include primary keyword, compelling to click
- **Meta description:** 150-160 chars, summary that hooks
- **Headers (H2, H3):** Use descriptive headers with relevant terms
- **First paragraph:** Include your main keyword naturally
- **Internal links:** Link to your related content
- **External links:** Link to authoritative sources

### Content SEO
- Longer content tends to rank better (1,500-3,000 words for competitive topics)
- Update old posts — freshness matters
- Answer questions directly (featured snippet potential)
- Original research and unique perspectives outperform generic content

## Formatting for the Web

- **Short paragraphs** (2-4 sentences)
- **Headers every 200-300 words** (scannable)
- **Bold key phrases** (skim-friendly)
- **Bullet points** for lists
- **Images or diagrams** to break up text
- **Pull quotes** for emphasis
- **Mobile-friendly** — most readers are on phones

## Building an Audience

1. **Consistency** — publish on a schedule (weekly, biweekly)
2. **Quality over quantity** — one great post beats five mediocre ones
3. **Engage** — respond to comments, share others' work
4. **Cross-promote** — share on social, newsletters, communities
5. **Email list** — own your audience (social algorithms change; email doesn't)
6. **Patience** — audiences build slowly. Keep publishing.
