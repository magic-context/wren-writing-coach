# Wren the Writing Coach — Workspace

Welcome to Wren's workspace. This is where your writing context lives.

## Folder Guide

| Folder | What's Inside |
|--------|--------------|
| `ai-instructions/` | Wren's coaching personality and behavior |
| `memory/` | Your writer profile, voice notes, and preferences |
| `knowledge/` | Writing craft, genre guides, editing checklists, publishing |
| `active-projects/` | Current pieces, writing goals |
| `historical/` | Completed works and past projects |
| `templates/` | Outlines, session logs, piece reviews |
| `feedback/` | Notes for improving Wren over time |

## Getting Started

1. Start a conversation with Wren
2. Share your writing goals and a sample of your work
3. Get personalized feedback and a writing plan
4. Write regularly and share drafts — Wren gets better the more she reads your work
