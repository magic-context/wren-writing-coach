# Wren — Your AI Writing Coach That Knows Your Voice

Every grammar tool checks your commas. Wren develops **your voice** — she remembers your style, your strengths, your patterns, and the specific things that make your writing yours. She gives feedback that makes you a better writer without making you sound like someone else.

## The Problem

You paste a draft into an AI and ask for feedback. It gives you generic advice: "use more active voice," "vary sentence length." Fine, but useless. It doesn't know you prefer short punchy sentences. It doesn't remember the great opening you wrote last week. It doesn't know you're working on a novel and this is chapter seven. Every. Single. Time.

**Wren fixes this.**

## What Wren Does

✍️ **Voice-Preserving Feedback** — Wren improves your writing without replacing it with hers. Your voice stays yours.

📝 **Craft Coaching** — Structure, clarity, style, and pacing — tied to your actual writing, not textbook examples

📚 **Genre-Specific Guidance** — Blog posts, technical docs, creative fiction, academic papers — each has its own rules and Wren knows them

✂️ **Structured Editing** — Four-pass editing process: structure → content → line editing → copy editing. Never nitpicks commas when structure is broken.

🔄 **Growth Tracking** — Wren notices patterns across your work. "Your openings have gotten much stronger." "You still hedge in conclusions."

📊 **Publishing Support** — Platform selection, SEO basics, formatting, and audience building

### The Magic Context Difference

Traditional AI is **stateless** — it forgets everything between conversations. Magic Context gives AI specialists **persistent memory** through structured workspaces. This means:

- **Session 1:** Wren reads your sample, identifies your voice, and learns your goals
- **Session 5:** Wren knows your strengths (great hooks) and weaknesses (transitions) and coaches accordingly
- **Session 20:** Wren tracks your voice evolution, references your best work as examples, and adjusts difficulty
- **Session 50+:** Wren is an editor who knows your writing as well as you do

**Your context is yours.** It's stored as plain markdown files you can read, edit, or export anytime. No black box. No vendor lock-in.

### First Session

Wren will guide you through a writer intake:
1. Your writing goals and experience
2. Genres you write (or want to write)
3. Your voice and style preferences
4. A writing sample (optional but valuable)
5. How you like to receive feedback

Then she'll start coaching based on your actual writing.

### Ongoing Use

- **Share drafts** for feedback at any editing level
- **Writing sessions** with brainstorming and outlining support
- **Craft lessons** tied to your specific weaknesses
- **Publishing guidance** when you're ready to ship
- **Progress reviews** to see how your writing has evolved
